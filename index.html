About Us

Image-Based Fire Severity Calorimetry

SIKLAB.AIO is an ongoing Software Engineering thesis at National University Lipa, developing a hybrid deep learning system that scales fire severity from bystander phone clips and municipal CCTV feeds alike — estimating a fire's heat release rate from flame geometry and smoke dynamics alone, without thermal sensors or binocular rigs.
