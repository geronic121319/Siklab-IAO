Siklab-AIO.org : Image-based Calorimetry Fire Severity Scaling Using Deep Learning Model

Bystanders already pull out their phones during a fire, and municipal CCTV is already watching the street. Siklab AI is a hybrid system that reads both — grading a fire's severity in real time and sending the nearest Bureau of Fire Protection station a visual intelligence report and GPS location before a human ever finishes dialing.

Who this is for

Turning one report into everyone's advantage

Fire dispatchers

Real-time severity and GPS telemetry before a truck rolls out, so personnel, apparatus, and water volume can be sized correctly the first time.

Residents

No hotline to memorize and no address to describe under panic just point the camera and let the app do the reporting.

LGUs & DRRMOs

A software-only alternative to installing physical sensor grids across every residential and commercial block.

About the research

Siklab AI is the subject of an ongoing Software Engineering thesis at National University Lipa, developing a hybrid deep learning system that scales fire severity from bystander phone clips and municipal CCTV feeds alike — estimating a fire's heat release rate from flame geometry and smoke dynamics alone, without thermal sensors or binocular rigs.
